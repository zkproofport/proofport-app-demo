# Scenario-specific credential demos

The user's September 5 revision supersedes the earlier uniform green design.
Apply `hallmark/skills/hallmark/SKILL.md` inside the existing Next.js architecture.
Retain Geist, Phosphor, native CSS, the proof hooks and the original All demos.

## Structure and typography

- Madang: photographic community composition, people and agents in a courtyard,
  a prominent 40px Upbit KYC requirement and an Upbit → GIWA → proof → access path.
- Common: a Coinbase KYC DeFi workspace with Supply/Borrow preview and an asset
  table. No fabricated yields, balances, deposits or financial operations.
- Borderless: a large exclusion statement and editable sample blacklist.
  Requests use exclusion mode, not inclusion. No claim of legal sanctions coverage.
- zk blind: topic sidebar, illustrative anonymous discussions and domain proof.
  No affiliation with Blind. No fabricated user counts or real posts.
- Korean ID: one explanatory screen with Ownership, Age check and Region choices.
  Every underlying circuit keeps its own request state and direct query URL.
- One persistent demo selector, edge-aligned service mastheads, simple footer.
- Display 52–112px desktop / 43–68px mobile; body 18–22px, form labels and helper
  text 16px. Readability takes priority over putting all content above the fold.
- Named theme tokens: courtyard greens, DeFi blue, blocklist rust, workplace
  charcoal, identity indigo. Light, dark and system modes are supported.
- Restrained press and hover feedback, immediate focus rings, reduced motion.

## Boundaries

All demos retains its original body and request code. Experimental qualifications
are preserved. Agent participation is an illustrative scenario, not an implemented
delegation protocol. Demo access is browser state, not server authorization.
Receiving a proof does not grant access before cryptographic verification.

## Madang benefits revision

GIWA alone now uses a benefits catalogue and membership pass. A compact 32–43px
heading replaces the oversized slogan. The featured dinner invitation leads,
followed by member-only airdrops and people/agent community access. The existing
courtyard image is supporting artwork inside the invitation. Benefits are concise, visible summaries; they do not make bookings, claims or agent authorizations. The
right-hand Upbit KYC pass retains the real proof flow. Other scenarios and
All demos are unchanged by this revision.

## Madang service identity

An original courtyard M mark and lowercase Madang wordmark now identify the
service header, membership pass, invitation and credential flow. SVG source:
`public/brand/madang-mark.svg`. The external Upbit mark was taken from the
favicon declared by upbit.com; existing GIWA and ZKProofport assets are reused.
Source provenance is recorded in `public/brand/README.md`; all logos are local.

The compact revision removes the redundant service navigation and benefit filters.
The proof button is titled Get my Madang pass. No additional account or wallet
connection is requested. The privacy copy distinguishes KYC eligibility from
personal identity and specifically names the KYC-linked wallet address, rather
than making an absolute claim that the app cannot see any address. Pre-verification
copy uses future tense; success wording still depends on cryptographic verification.

their agents. Invitations, airdrops and connections sit beside private access.
